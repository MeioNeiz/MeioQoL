# Meio QoL

## 1.1.0

- Group invites: a line above Accept and Decline shows the inviter's level, class and zone.
  Decline invites (off by default) turns strangers away with a chat line and your reason;
  friends, guild and group finder invites still pop up
- Summons are accepted 5 seconds after you are out of combat, with one chat line saying who
  and where; click Cancel meanwhile to stay
- Restock from your bank: opening it moves what's missing of your restock items into your
  bags
- A warning on screen and in chat when water, reagents or ammo on your restock list drop to a
  quarter
- Instant flight map, buy many at once (Shift-click: Stack and MAX in the amount box) and a
  wider vendor window with 20 items a page
- Off until you turn them on: auto repair from your gold, skip bind-on-pickup prompts,
  farther camera, fishing sounds, auto quest accept and turn-in (skips dailies, repeatables,
  reward choices and quests that cost gold)
- Beside FlareUI, its copies of these stay in charge until you type `/qol takeover`;
  `/qol flare` gives them back

## 1.0.0

- First release: skips an NPC's only chat option, keeps chosen items topped up at vendors,
  repairs from guild funds, accepts resurrections from your group and loots everything at
  once without the loot window
